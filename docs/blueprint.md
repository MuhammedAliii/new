# **App Name**: Better Call Hana Landing

## Core Features:

- Branded Header & Navigation: Display the 'Better Call Hana' logo and key navigation links including 'How it Works', 'Practice Impact', 'Contact', and a 'Book Demo' button, all centrally aligned.
- Hero Section Showcase: Prominently feature the main headline, subheadline, and dual Call-to-Action buttons ('Hear Hana in Action', 'Book Demo') to immediately engage visitors.
- Practice Impact Simulation: Visually demonstrate the financial benefits and problem/solution using a sleek two-column comparison with 'Without Hana' and 'With Hana' cards, concluding with potential growth and a 'Calculate Your ROI' CTA.
- How It Works Overview: Provide a simplified, step-by-step explanation of the AI voice receptionist's process using three minimalist horizontal cards.
- Lead Capture/Contact Section: Offer a minimalist section allowing users to easily get in touch or book a meeting for inquiries and demos.
- Branded Footer: Conclude the page with a centered footer displaying the standalone 'H' logo icon, essential contact details, and copyright information.

## Style Guidelines:

- Color Palette Foundation: Adhere strictly to a premium, sophisticated achromatic palette using pure white (#FFFFFF) and very light off-white/cream (#FBFBF8) for backgrounds, with deep charcoal gray (#2D2D2D) for all primary text and call-to-action buttons. No bright corporate blues are used.
- Primary Color (Interactive Elements): Deep Charcoal Gray (#2D2D2D), used for prominent call-to-action buttons, creating strong contrast against light backgrounds and conveying seriousness.
- Background Colors: Main backgrounds utilize pure white (#FFFFFF) for cleanliness and very light off-white/cream (#FBFBF8) for subtle section differentiation, contributing to a spacious, minimalist feel.
- Accent Color: A very dark, desaturated cool gray (#242428) is introduced as a subtle accent, providing minimal differentiation and depth for subtle elements like borders or secondary interactive states, maintaining a sophisticated and understated tone without introducing vibrant hues.
- Headline and Body Text Font: 'Inter' (sans-serif) for its modern, highly readable, and clean aesthetic, suitable for both headlines and body text to maintain consistency and clarity across the page.
- Minimalist Icons: Utilize subtle, minimalist line icons such as a red 'X' and a deep charcoal checkmark to visually enhance content sections like the Practice Impact Simulation, aligning with the sleek design philosophy.
- Grid & Spacing: Employ a clean, centered layout approach with generous white space around elements and content blocks, enhancing readability and contributing to a premium, uncluttered user experience. Key sections like 'Practice Impact' will use a structured two-column layout with rounded cards, while 'How It Works' will feature a horizontal card arrangement.
- Subtle Interactions: Implement discreet animations for interactive elements, such as elegant hover effects on buttons and navigation links, to provide polished feedback without detracting from the minimalist design.
